How Member 360 works, in plain language. Open a section to read it.
What Member 360 Is For

Member 360 is MISMO's membership desk. It holds every member and prospect organization, the people at each one, where each organization sits in its renewal cycle, and the notes and follow-ups the team keeps.

It also sends MISMO's branded membership emails. You pick who gets an email, see it exactly as they will see it, and press Send. The email goes out from your own Outlook, so replies come back to you.

Aptify is still the official record for memberships, levels and payments. Member 360 is MISMO's working layer on top of it: stages, owners, holds, conversations and follow-ups are saved here and are not lost when the member data is refreshed.

Where the Member Information Comes From

The member list is exported from Aptify and loaded into Member 360. The date it was last loaded is shown at the bottom of the menu on the left.

Levels, membership dates, certifications and webinars are refreshed each week from three Aptify exports, uploaded under Settings, then Aptify Member Data (see The Weekly Aptify Pull). Everything the team has added here is kept: notes, stages, owners, holds, follow-ups and prospects.

Two things are worth checking after a refresh, both under Members and Prospects: organizations that share a Company ID, and organizations with no Company ID. Either one can cause an email to go to the wrong place or not go at all.

What Stops an Email Going Out

An organization drops out of the automated run when it has renewed, is lost, has a payment in process, is on hold, or has been routed to a person.

Routing to a person means MISMO handles that renewal by hand instead. Any one of these sends an organization down that route. They can be switched on or off under Settings, in Hand-Off Rules.

Sending a Playbook Email

Start in Outreach Planner. Anything due in the next seven days appears there, grouped by email, with a Send button on each group.

  1. Press the Send button on the group you want.
  2. A panel opens showing the email and everyone it would go to. Tick or untick people. The panel says who it will come from.
  3. Use Preview For to see the email filled in for any one person on the list.
  4. Press Send Me a Test first if you want it in your own inbox before anyone else gets it.
  5. Press Send. Press it a second time to confirm.

You can also send from Renewals or from an organization's own record, which is the usual route when you are handling one member rather than a batch.

The panel will not let you send while the wording still has text in square brackets, or while the email has no wording at all.

Testing an Email before It Goes Out

There are two test buttons and they do slightly different things.

From the Playbooks tab

Every email has a Send Me a Test button next to Save Wording. It sends that email to you alone, filled in with a real member as the sample, so the merge fields show real values.

It uses whatever is in the Subject and Body boxes at that moment, not the last saved version. So you can change the wording, test it, change it again, and only press Save Wording when you are happy.

From the Send panel

The Send Me a Test button there fills the email in using the first person you have ticked.

Either way the test goes only to you, nobody on the list receives anything, and nothing is recorded as sent.

Editing the Wording and Merge Fields

Open Playbooks, open the email you want, change the Subject Line or Email Body, then press Save Wording. The change applies to every send of that email from then on. The card shows who last edited it and when.

Merge Fields

Anything in double braces is a placeholder. It is swapped for that person's own details as the email goes out, so one piece of wording serves everyone.

Square Brackets

Text in square brackets is a note to yourself that something still has to be filled in. The app refuses to send while any remain, which is deliberate.

Bullet Lists

Start a line with a dash or a bullet character and the whole paragraph becomes a bulleted list in the email.

The timings and the set of nine emails are fixed in the app. Wording can be changed here at any time; adding a tenth email or moving a date is a change to the app itself.

Who the Email Comes From

Four people can send from Member 360: Amy Moses, Kelly Bradshaw, Brian Vieaux and Jonna Critchley. The email goes out from whoever pressed Send, through their own Outlook, so replies land in their inbox.

The Send panel always states who it will come from before you press anything. If it names the wrong person, you are signed in as the wrong person.

Sending only works on the resources.mismo.org copy of Member 360 and only for those four people. If you do not see a Send button, that is why.

What Happens after You Press Send

Member 360 does not send email itself. Pressing Send writes one job, called a batch, into MISMO's private store. A Power Automate flow picks the batch up, sends each email from the right person's Outlook, and writes the result back.

  1. You press Send. Member 360 saves the batch: the email, everyone on the list, and each person's own details.
  2. Saving the batch wakes the flow, usually within a few seconds.
  3. The flow checks the sender is one of the four, then sends the emails one at a time, about three seconds apart, so Outlook does not throttle them.
  4. The flow records the result. Member 360 shows it on the card: sending, then sent, with the count.

A batch of fifty takes around three minutes. The app shows Sending now while the flow is working. If it has been more than fifteen minutes it will say so, and that is worth flagging.

Because the whole email is built before it is handed over, what you saw in the preview is exactly what the member receives.

Behind the Scenes

This section is for whoever maintains Member 360. Nothing here is needed to use it day to day.

The Pieces

PieceWhat It Does
The appOne file, index.html, in the public GitHub repository GitMISMO/member-360, served at resources.mismo.org/member-360. It holds no member data and no passwords.
Sign-inThe shared MISMO Resources sign-in, the same one every MISMO tool uses. It lasts four hours.
The save relayThe one door through which the app reads and writes data. Every MISMO tool uses it with its own project key; this one is member-360.
The data storeThe private GitHub repository GitMISMO/member-360-data. All member data, notes, settings and email wording live here, never in the app file.
The flowA Power Automate flow named Member 360 Emails. It is the only thing that actually sends email.

How a Send Travels

  1. Pressing Send writes one batch into data/outbox.json in the data repository, through the relay.
  2. That write fires a GitHub webhook, which starts the flow.
  3. The flow reads the batch, checks the sender against its list of four, and sends each email from that person's Outlook connection, with a three second pause between each.
  4. The flow writes the outcome into data/outbox-sent.json, which only the flow writes to.

The flow swaps each person's values into the email as it sends. The app has already built the email itself, including the branded shell and the benefits list, so the flow changes nothing about how it looks.

Changing the App

The file is edited directly on GitHub, or a new copy is uploaded. If uploading, download the current copy from GitHub first and change that. Uploading an older copy silently undoes everything changed since.

Then upload all three in Member 360: open Settings, then Aptify Member Data, click Choose the Files, pick the three files, and click Upload Aptify Files. Member 360 cleans them up itself; Claude isn't needed. A file left out keeps last week's.

What Each File Is Used For

  • Terms: each member's level and Membership Ends date, and whether the membership is current. Aptify is the record, so these replace what Member 360 had.
  • Companies: only the company type (Credit Union sets the Credit Union flag) and the city and state when a record has no address.
  • Orders: CMSP and AMSP enrollments, renewals and exams (a $0 one is a free certification against the level's allowance) and monthly webinar registrations, by the company billed.

A membership in Aptify with no Member 360 record, and a current member with no active term, are listed in the Issues Log for someone to look at. Member 360 never turns a member Former on its own.

Two Things That Look Right and Are Not

The Companies table has Join Date, Dues Paid Thru, Status and Member Type. Those are MBA's membership, not MISMO's. A company can read Non-Member there and still be a MISMO member in good standing. Using those dates would put the wrong renewal date on most of the list.

MISMO Member Type Name is a sticky flag. Companies that stopped paying in 2006 still carry it. It tells you the level, never whether the membership is live. Only the term dates in the second file decide that.

Rebuilding the Views from Scratch

For each one: open the service, click More, click Create View, type the name, click OK, then click the gear icon to set the fields and filters.

Member 360 - Companies, under Membership then Companies.

  • Filter: MISMO Member Type Name, Is Not Blank
  • Fields to add: DuesPaidThru, Original Join Date, Join Date, Termination Date, Associate Member Level, City, State, Web Site. The rest are there by default.

Member 360 - MISMO Membership Terms, under Order Entry then Subscriptions.

  • Filter 1: Product, In List, 67583,67584,67585,67586,67587
  • No date filter. The view lists every MISMO membership term, current and past, so Member 360 can show each company's history from Aptify. Only terms that haven't ended set who is a current member.
  • Fields to add: Subscriber Company, which is the Company ID that joins to the other file, and Status.

Member 360 - MISMO Orders, under Order Entry then Orders. It is a copy of the view MISMO Orders with Batch, with one filter added: Line1_ProductCategory is MISMO. It asks for a date range each time it runs: use January 1 of this year through today. Aptify shows only the first line of each order, so an order with MISMO on a later line isn't in the file.

Do not change a view you did not create. MISMO Orders with Batch was left as it was. There is an older view called mismo membership term, built by Perry Williams as a one-company lookup. It is not an export and returns nothing.

The Weekly Pull, Step by Step

Do this once a week. It takes a few minutes.

  1. Sign in to Aptify yourself at aptify.mba.org.
  2. Open Membership, then Companies, then the view called Member 360 - Companies. Click Actions, then Export to Excel. File name: mismo-member-companies.
  3. Open Order Entry, then Subscriptions, then the view called Member 360 - MISMO Membership Terms. Click Actions, then Export to Excel. File name: mismo-membership-terms.
  4. Open Order Entry, then Orders, then the view called Member 360 - MISMO Orders. When it asks for dates, use January 1 of this year through today. Click Actions, then Export to Excel. File name: mismo-orders.
  5. In Member 360, open Settings, then Aptify Member Data. Click Choose the Files, pick all three files, and click Upload Aptify Files.
